# Plan: CHOMPI-PIPES delay-level modulation destination

Add a seventh Modulation-mode destination: GLOBAL delay-level (delay mix/feedback) modulation, selected with SHIFT + black key 9 (G#4), shown in yellow. It mirrors the distortion modulation exactly: one shared 0..32 amount, adjusted by the leftmost pipe encoder (encoder 1) only, swept around the user's delay setting by a free-running drift modulator, stored in the SD preset as a new global `delay_mod` field, and settable over MIDI with a new CC. Per the requester's decisions: the drift sweeps the FULL combined 0..100 delay control (mix region AND feedback/self-oscillation region), and both the distortion modulation and the delay modulation have MIDI CCs (distortion already has CC 115; delay gets CC 116, receive-only like the rest of the extended map).

## What the firmware actually supports today (checked in the code)

- **Six destinations exist; key 9 is free.** [ModDest.h](../code/src/ModDest.h) defines `enum ModDest` with `kModDestAttack .. kModDestDriveMod` and `kNumModDests` ([ModDest.h](../code/src/ModDest.h#L29-L38)). The destination-key table `kModDestKeySw[kNumModDests] = {7, 12, 13, 14, 21, 29}` maps physical black keys 1-5 and 8; keys 6, 7, 9 and 10 select nothing ([ModDest.h](../code/src/ModDest.h#L48-L52), `ModDestAfterKey`). Key 8 (F#4, `KEY_23`) is the distortion destination, so key 9 (G#4) is the next black key to the right.
- **How the distortion modulation works (the template to mirror):**
  - Amount state: `drive_mod_step_` (0..`kMaxModSteps`=32), a free-running `DriftModulator drive_mod_` and `drive_mod_target_` in [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L1264-L1271); setters/getters `SetDriveModStep`/`GetDriveModStep` clamp to 0..32 ([OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L516-L522)).
  - DSP: once per block, `drive_mod_.Advance()` then `drive_mod_target_ = DriftModulator::ApplyWindow(drive_target_, step/32, drive_mod_.Value())` and `distortion_.PrepareBlock(drive_mod_target_)`; per sample `fonepole(drive_, drive_mod_target_, .001f)` slews it click-free ([OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L862-L868)). Amount 0 is bit-exact unmodulated.
  - Encoder: in Modulation Mode, `mod_dest_ == kModDestDriveMod` is handled BEFORE the per-pipe switch; only pipe encoder 1 (`p == 0`) adjusts it, the other three are inert, SHIFT+turn = plain turn, no CC sent ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L1637-L1648)).
  - Knob LEDs: only PTH 1 shows the amount in the destination color; knobs 2-4 are forced off ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L2435-L2441) and the `kModDestDriveMod` case at [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L2459-L2461)).
  - Value bar: `kModBarDriveMod = 9` + `kModDestBarPage[kNumModDests]` map ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L370-L386)), drawn red (`kBarDrive`) from `GetDriveModStep()` ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L2216-L2223)).
  - Destination-key LEDs: the loop `for(d = 0; d < kNumModDests; d++)` lights every key in `kModDestKeySw` at 100% (selected) / 75% in its `ModDestColor`, and forces all other black-key LEDs off - fully table-driven, so a seventh entry just works ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L2389-L2410)).
  - `chompi_main.cpp` static_asserts pin `kModDestKeySw[]` to `Hardware::SwId::KEY_16..KEY_23` ([chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp#L173-L186)).
- **The delay control to modulate:** `delay_mix_percent_` 0..100 is the combined control - 0..50 sweeps mix 0..50% (0% exact dry), above 50 the mix locks at 50% and feedback rises 35%..100% into bounded self-oscillation; the pivot mapping lives in `SetDelayMixPercent` ([OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L187-L199), [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L634-L656)). `StereoBbdDelay` ramps `mix_`/`feedback_` toward their targets per sample, so block-rate target changes glide click-free ([StereoBbdDelay.h](../code/src/StereoBbdDelay.h#L153-L158)). The wet path runs at [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h#L1122).
- **Presets:** `OrganPreset` has the global `uint8_t drive_mod` field (0..32, default 0) at [OrganPreset.h](../code/src/OrganPreset.h#L86-L88), serialized as `"drive_mod":%u` in `Serialize`, parsed with `QueryInt(..., kDefaultMod, 0, kMaxMod)` ([OrganPreset.h](../code/src/OrganPreset.h#L300-L304)), and wired in `CaptureFromEngine`/`ApplyToEngine` ([OrganPreset.h](../code/src/OrganPreset.h#L310-L337), [OrganPreset.h](../code/src/OrganPreset.h#L342-L370)). Missing keys get defaults and unknown keys are ignored, so the version stays 1 and old/new files inter-operate.
- **MIDI:** the distortion modulation already has `kDriveModCc = 115` in [MidiCcMap.h](../code/src/MidiCcMap.h#L44), handled receive-only in [MidiHandler.h](../code/src/MidiHandler.h#L190-L194). The map "ends at CC 115, staying clear of the reserved channel-mode CCs 120-127", so CC 116 is the next free number. The header comment states the extended CCs are receive-only (nothing is sent back out).
- **Tests:** `mod_dest_test` covers `ModDestAfterKey` per key ([mod_dest_test.cpp](../code/tests/mod_dest_test.cpp)); `organ_preset_test` round-trips `drive_mod` through serialize/parse and capture/apply with a fake engine ([organ_preset_test.cpp](../code/tests/organ_preset_test.cpp#L437-L459)); `midi_cc_map_test` covers the CC map; all run via [tests/Makefile](../code/tests/Makefile#L11).
- **Key-9 SwId (to verify during implementation):** `kModDestKeySw[5] = 29 = KEY_23` is F#4; [hardware.h](../code/src/hardware.h#L82-L84) lists `KEY_23 (U9 6)`, `KEY_24 (U9 7)`, `KEY_25 (U9 8)` consecutively, so black key 9 (G#4) is almost certainly `KEY_24` and key 10 (A#4) `KEY_25` - confirm against the key-scan comments before writing the static_assert.

## Steps

### Phase 1 - Seventh destination in the selection/UI layer

1. [ModDest.h](../code/src/ModDest.h): add `kModDestDelayMod` after `kModDestDriveMod` (so `kNumModDests` becomes 7); append key 9's SwId (`KEY_24`, expected 30) to `kModDestKeySw`; extend `ModDestAfterKey` with the `kModDestKeySw[6] -> kModDestDelayMod` case; add a `kModDestDelayMod` case to `ModDestColor` returning yellow (r=1, g=1, b=0 - the delay color used by the ENC_6 delay page and delay bar). Update the file-header comment: key 9 selects the delay modulation (yellow, global, encoder 1 only), only keys 6, 7 and 10 stay inert, and the amount IS stored in presets as the global `delay_mod` field.
2. [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp): add the matching `static_assert(kModDestKeySw[6] == static_cast<int>(Hardware::SwId::KEY_24), ...)` next to the existing six.
3. [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp): add `kModBarDelayMod = 10` and append it to `kModDestBarPage`.
4. [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp) encoder handling (~L1637): extend the global-destination early-out to `mod_dest_ == kModDestDriveMod || mod_dest_ == kModDestDelayMod` (or add a parallel branch): only `p == 0` calls `engine.SetDelayModStep(engine.GetDelayModStep() + delta)`; bar via `kModDestBarPage[mod_dest_]`; other encoders inert; SHIFT+turn = plain turn; no CC sent (mirrors drive mod).
5. [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp) value-bar draw (~L2216): add a `bar_page_ == kModBarDelayMod` branch: `lit = (engine.GetDelayModStep() * kNumWhiteKeys + 16) / OrganEngine::kMaxModSteps`, `kind = kBarDelay` (the yellow delay bar already used by the ENC_6 delay page).
6. [chompi_main.cpp](../code/src/chompi_main.cpp) knob LEDs (~L2435): extend the "knobs 2-4 off" guard to the delay destination and add a `kModDestDelayMod` case to the step switch: `step = engine.GetDelayModStep(); max = OrganEngine::kMaxModSteps;` - PTH 1 then shows the amount in yellow via `ModDestColor`.
7. [mod_dest_test.cpp](../code/tests/mod_dest_test.cpp): assert key 9 (`30`) maps to `kModDestDelayMod`, keys 6/7/10 leave the destination unchanged, and update any `kNumModDests`-sized expectations.

### Phase 2 - Engine DSP

8. [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h): add state mirroring the drive mod: `uint8_t delay_mod_step_` (boot 0), `DriftModulator delay_mod_` with its own seed (drive uses `0xC3D2E1F0u` - pick a different constant), `SetDelayModStep`/`GetDelayModStep` clamped to `kMaxModSteps`, init in `Init()` next to `drive_mod_step_ = 0`.
9. [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h): extract the pivot mapping from `SetDelayMixPercent` into a private helper, e.g. `ApplyDelayCombined(float percent)`: 0..50 -> `delay_.SetMix(percent * .01f)`, `delay_.SetFeedback(kDelayFeedbackFloor)`; 50..100 -> mix locked at `kDelayMixAtPivot`, feedback `kDelayFeedbackFloor + ...` exactly as today. `SetDelayMixPercent(int)` keeps clamping/storing `delay_mix_percent_` and delegates to the helper.
10. [OrganEngine.h](../code/src/OrganEngine.h) block processing (next to the drive-mod block at ~L862): `delay_mod_.Advance(num_samples)`, then compute the modulated combined percent `p = DriftModulator::ApplyWindow(delay_mix_percent_ * .01f, delay_mod_step_ * (1.f/kMaxModSteps), delay_mod_.Value()) * 100.f` and call `ApplyDelayCombined(p)`. Amount 0 must be bit-exact the user's setting (ApplyWindow's exact-center property, same as drive). The per-sample ramps inside `StereoBbdDelay` smooth the block-rate steps. When the base setting is in the feedback region the drift now sweeps through self-oscillation - the intended "full combined 0..100" behavior.
    - Note: modulating the mix while the delay tail is ringing changes the wet level live; that is the requested "delay level modulation".

## Risks / open questions

- **Self-oscillation drift:** the requester chose the full 0..100 sweep, so a high base delay setting plus modulation can drift through the feedback region up to the bounded max (`kDelayFeedbackMax`, soft-clipped by `StereoBbdDelay`). It cannot run away, but it can get loud - worth an ears-on check in step 17.
- **Three yellow destinations:** release, pipe-length modulation and now delay modulation all use yellow. Only the selected key shows 100%, and the destinations live on different keys, but the colors are no longer unique. Accepted per the request ("in yellow of course"); flag if a distinct hue (e.g. amber) is wanted.
- **Key-9 SwId assumption:** the plan assumes G#4 = `KEY_24` from the consecutive `KEY_23/24/25` entries in [hardware.h](../code/src/hardware.h#L82-L84); confirm before writing the static_assert in step 2, or the destination will land on the wrong physical key.
- **CC direction:** the extended map is receive-only by convention, so turning encoder 1 sends no CC for either modulation. If CC *out* on encoder turns is wanted later, that is a separate change (new coalesced-dirty plumbing in `chompi_main.cpp`), not part of this plan.
- **DSP cost:** one extra `DriftModulator.Advance()` per block plus the existing `StereoBbdDelay` ramps - negligible, same pattern as the drive mod.
